About LifeLine

Connecting lives through organ and blood donation across India

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between patients in critical need of organs, blood, and blood products, and the generous donors and hospitals willing to help. We believe that timely access to accurate information can save lives.

Our Vision

A world where no patient dies waiting for an organ or blood transfusion. Where every donation finds its recipient quickly and efficiently, and where the gift of life is accessible to all who need it.

Real-Time Matching

Our intelligent matching algorithm instantly connects compatible donors with patients based on blood group compatibility, organ type, urgency level, and geographic proximity.

Verified & Secure

All hospitals and medical facilities are verified. Patient and donor information is protected with bank-level encryption. We comply with all relevant medical data privacy regulations.
