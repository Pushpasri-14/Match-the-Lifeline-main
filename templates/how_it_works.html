How LifeLine Works

Simple steps to connect donors with patients in need

1

Register Your Account

Sign up as a Patient, Donor, or Hospital. Provide your details, blood group, and contact information. Hospitals need to provide license verification.

2

Post Need or Donation

Patients post urgent requirements for organs, blood, plasma, serum, or platelets. Donors/hospitals post available donations including brain-dead organ donations.

3

Automatic Matching

Our smart algorithm instantly finds compatible matches based on blood group, organ type, urgency, location, and medical compatibility.

4

Get Notified

Both parties receive instant notifications when a match is found. View detailed medical information and contact details securely.

5

Respond & Coordinate

Donors can accept or decline matches. Accepted matches connect both parties directly for hospital coordination and logistics.

6

Save a Life

Complete the donation process. Mark matches as fulfilled. Track your impact and help build a community of life-savers.

For Patients & Families

You can post needs for: Organs (heart, liver, kidney, lung, pancreas, intestine, cornea, skin, bone, heart valve), Blood, Plasma, Platelets, and Serum. Include patient medical details, hospital information, urgency level, and contact information.

All hospitals are verified during registration. For organ donations, the donor must be declared brain dead by a certified medical team. You'll see hospital verification badges and doctor certification details on each donation listing.

You'll receive a notification with the donor's contact details. You can directly coordinate with the hospital and medical team. Once the donation is completed, mark the match as fulfilled to help others.

For Donors & Hospitals

Living donors can donate: Blood, Plasma, Platelets, Serum, Kidney (in some cases), Liver segment.
Deceased/Brain-dead donors (registered by hospitals): Heart, Liver, Kidneys, Lungs, Pancreas, Intestine, Corneas, Skin, Bone, Heart Valves.

When a patient is declared brain dead by a certified medical team, the hospital can register the available organs on LifeLine. The system automatically matches with waiting patients. The hospital coordinates the retrieval and transplant process. This is a critical, time-sensitive process - organs must be retrieved within hours.

The system matches based on medical compatibility (blood group, organ type, urgency, location). When a match is found, you can review the patient's details and choose to accept or decline. You cannot directly select a specific patient, but you can respond to each match request.
